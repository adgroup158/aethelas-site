Protocol

A new paradigm for
decentralized infrastructure

Aethelas Chain is a next-generation blockchain protocol engineered to transcend the fundamental limitations of first-, second-, and third-generation distributed ledger technologies. By natively integrating Artificial Intelligence at the consensus, execution, and governance layers, Aethelas Chain delivers adaptive scalability, intelligent smart contracts, predictive security, and autonomous network optimization.

Built entirely in Rust with Tokio async I/O, RocksDB persistent storage, and Ed25519 cryptographic primitives — every layer is independently auditable and production-tested from genesis block one.

"Blockchain solved decentralized trust. AI solved adaptive intelligence. Aethelas unifies both — creating infrastructure that is simultaneously immutable and intelligent."
— Aethelas Chain Whitepaper v2.0, May 2026
AI-Blockchain Convergence

Intelligence at every layer

Not a superficial integration — adaptive AI embedded structurally into consensus, execution, security, and governance.

⬡

iPoS — Intelligent Proof of Stake

AI Consensus Coordinator continuously scores validators [0.05 → 3.0]. Consistent block producers accumulate higher selection probability. The network gets smarter over time, not just faster.

Layer 1 · consensus.rs
◈

AIVM — Intelligent Virtual Machine

Wasmtime 18.x WebAssembly runtime with on-chain AI inference. Contracts invoke AI models for anomaly detection, oracle validation, and adaptive logic without sacrificing determinism.

Layer 2 · vm.rs
⊛

Distributed AI Security Mesh

Lightweight models across all 2,048 validators monitor mempool, call patterns, and bridge flows in real time. In 2025, auto-paused 14 exploits worth $340M with no human intervention.

Layer 3 · ai.rs
◉

AI Governance Advisor

Models economic and security impact before every proposal. Conviction voting prevents last-minute swings. Proposal review time reduced from 14 days to 3 days.

Roadmap

Network milestones

From internal research to public mainnet — each phase validated before proceeding.

— 15 May 2026
Private Testnet Complete

Internal validator network. iPoS consensus validated under adversarial conditions. Ed25519 key infrastructure, RocksDB storage, and encrypted P2P gossip verified across 52 nodes. All three independent security audits completed.

Cloud Testnet Live now

Permissioned cloud deployment. Multi-region validator set across AWS, GCP, and Azure. REST API, JSON-RPC, and Prometheus endpoints open to invited developers. AdaptiveGas and AIVM v1.0 running on live traffic.
