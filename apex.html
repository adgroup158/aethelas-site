The problem

Regulators demand proof.
AI cannot provide it.

GDPR Art. 17

A data subject requests erasure. The model already learned from their data. Deleting the row does not delete the learning.

EU AI Act

High-risk systems must evidence training data provenance and post-market monitoring — on demand, to a supervisor.

DPDP Act 2023

India-specific erasure and consent obligations, now entering enforcement, with no accepted technical means of proof.

Today the answer to “prove it” is a signed statement from the vendor.

There is no independent, tamper-evident record of what an AI system learned, or of what it was made to forget. That gap is what Aethelas closes.

What we build

Four operations, each cryptographically recorded

Learn

Every learning event is written as a signed, timestamped ledger entry — who contributed what data, when, and to which model version.

Remember

Tiered memory with a temporal graph. Retrieval is reconstructable and attributable to specific recorded events.

Prove

Merkle inclusion proofs let a third party verify any claim about the training history without trusting the operator.

Unlearn

Redact a contributor’s entries and deterministically replay. Recall on their patterns drops; unrelated task knowledge is preserved.

Honest scope: the ledger’s value is provenance and auditable erasure — not higher model accuracy. Our own experiments show a small accuracy cost, and we report it.
Architecture

Why this needs a purpose-built chain

The constraint

Proof records are generated at learning-event frequency — orders of magnitude above any public chain’s block rate. Anchoring each one directly to a public ledger is neither economic nor fast enough.

Aethelas therefore runs its own consensus for high-frequency recording, and batches proof roots outward for public settlement.

We publish our limitations. It is the same discipline we ask regulated customers to trust us with.
Proposed integration

Cardano as the public anchor for AI audit trails

01 · Batch

Aethelas records memory operations at learning-event frequency and batches them.

02 · Commit

A Merkle root is computed over each batch inside the Aethelas node.

03 · Anchor

That root is submitted to Cardano as transaction metadata — no Plutus contract required.

04 · Verify

Any third party checks a proof against the Cardano transaction, without running an Aethelas node.

Value to the ecosystem

An enterprise, compliance-driven use case for Cardano metadata — recurring transaction volume tied to audit cycles rather than market sentiment, plus an open-source verifier any Cardano project can reuse.

What we are not proposing

Not migrating to Cardano. Not Cardano-native. Cardano is not a settlement layer for Aethelas consensus. Phase 1 requires no Plutus contracts and no protocol change on either side.

Market

Who buys this, and why now

Banking

RBI model-risk and audit obligations are handled manually today. AI adoption is outpacing the supervisory evidence available.

Healthcare

Patient erasure requests collide with models trained on patient data. No accepted method exists to evidence removal.

Government

Public-sector AI procurement increasingly requires auditability as a contract condition, not a nice-to-have.
